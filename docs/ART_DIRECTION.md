# HIVE — art direction

HIVE is MEADOW's sibling: the same hand, a new world. Everything here is measured against MEADOW's "Illustrated aerial references" (see `meadow/docs/REFERENCE_ANALYSIS.md`) and against the short screen recording of MEADOW (`sheep.mov`, the author's viral clip) that inspired this version: a painted tile on warm paper, a blue selection box with a world-unit label, a cream pill of contextual actions under the selected thing.

## What carries over unchanged
- **Tile on paper.** A rounded rectangle of painted ground on `#f4efe4`, its edge thinning into dry-brush specks. The garden is an object on a page.
- **One painted light.** MEADOW's `paintLight`: a warm lit side and a cool lavender shade (`#bdb9e3`). Sun high, upper left, slightly in front; shadows fall lower right. One sun shadow map for everything: flowers, bees, the hive, the hedge.
- **Gouache texture.** Flat-ish fields with soft internal gradients, long dry-brush strokes, a fine pigment stipple, pale flecks. No photo textures, no gloss, no black.
- **Interface.** Inter Tight + Fraunces, wordmark top left, cream pill toolbar, blue selection box with a unit label, a contextual bar under the selection.

## What is new
- **A cottage garden.** Lawn in MEADOW's greens with faint mowing stripes and clover; dark loamy beds (warm umber, raked furrows, crumbs, a spade-cut edge); a pale gravel path that runs from the front edge up to the hive's gravel apron; stepping stones; a low clipped hedge across the back; an apple tree; a fern with a ladybird; a terracotta water dish; a sage watering can; a snail on a stone.
- **The skep.** Woven straw coils with binding stitches, a dark arched doorway over a little landing board. Honey shows as amber soaking up the straw from the base and a drip that forms under the board.
- **Flowers are the heroes.** Modelled petals (parametric surfaces with a width profile, cup, curl and ruffle), never cards. Deeper colour at the heart, paler to the tip, a few painted veins, a dusty pollen centre. Stems are painted tubes; leaves are tapered ribbons with a folded midrib. Palette: cream, butter yellow, soft coral, dusty rose, lavender, cornflower blue, one deep magenta.
- **Bees are characters.** MEADOW's puff fields at a small scale: a round gold body with soft charcoal bands, a dark head, big eyes with a catchlight, short bobbing antennae, six legs that gather gold pollen; wings crisp and veined at rest, a soft shimmering fan in flight. No smiles, no teeth.

## Rules learned from MEADOW
- Shade on light colours (petals, fuzz) is mixed toward a neutral grey-lavender, or pink reads purple.
- Characters are drawn larger than life: blooms ~50–90 px across at 1440×900, bees ~16–24 px with wings.
- Shadows never arrive before the thing that casts them.
- Floating things (butterflies) keep away from the subject.
- Square hash cells read as digital: specks, crumbs and freckles are round.
